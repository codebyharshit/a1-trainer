der · die · das — Wörter

The most frequent German nouns, with article, plural, meaning and the reason for the article in brackets. Colours follow your book: der · die · das. Switch on Quiz to hide the articles — wrong answers are saved to your mistakes.

What the reasons mean
→ der
  • male male people & animals
  • day · month · season · daytime · weather · direction
  • -en (not a verb): Garten · -er people/tools · -ling -ismus -ist -or -ant -ent -ig -ich -eur
→ die
  • female · -in
  • -e ~90%
  • -ung -heit -keit -schaft -ion -tät -kunft 100%
  • -ik -ie -ei -ur -enz -anz -age -ade -ette -a
→ das
  • -chen -lein 100%
  • Ge- · verb (das Essen)
  • -um -ment -ma -o -zeug -nis -tum -il -ett
  • foreign loanwords (tendency)
Special
  • compound the LAST part decides (← base word shown)
  • exception breaks a rule
  • two genders meaning changes with the article
  • learn no reliable rule — learn it with the word
